Privacy Policy

Work Hours Tracker · Last updated October 13, 2026

What this app does

Work Hours Tracker is a personal stopwatch for logging work hours. You sign in with your Google account, track time, and review your hours by day, week and month.

What data is stored

The app stores only your work sessions: the date, start time, end time and duration of each session you save. Nothing else is collected. There is no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

Where your data lives

Your sessions are saved in a private application folder inside your own Google Drive (the Drive appDataFolder). This folder is invisible in the Drive interface and can be accessed only by this app acting on your behalf. Your data never passes through our servers, because there are none. Each Google account that signs in sees only its own data.

What we access

The app requests a single Google Drive permission (drive.appdata): the ability to manage its own application data in your Drive. It cannot see, read or modify any of your other Drive files.

Data sharing

We do not share, sell or disclose your data to anyone, because we never have access to it in the first place.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual sessions or clear everything from inside the app. You can also revoke the app's access at any time from your Google account permissions, which stops all future access.
